            Hi, this is Parrot.
            
          
            Walk anywhere and record up to 5 minutes of your journey: your voice and the sounds around you.
            
          
            Later, people who walk the same streets hear what you left behind, at the spot where you recorded it. Turn towards a sound to hear it more clearly.
            
          
            Put on headphones. In the concept, the phone rides in a parrot on your shoulder.
            
        
            

        
            
          
            Three permissions
            
          
            Your phone asks for these when you tap Start New Walk:
            
          
              
            
            • Motion & orientation — which way you face and when you take a step.
              • 
            
            • Microphone — records your walk, surroundings included.
              • 
            
            • Location — places each sound where it was recorded.
              • 
          
            
          
            Walks are private until you choose to share them, and the first 30 m of every walk are left out so your starting point stays yours.
            
        
            

        
            
          
            Keep your walks
            
          
            Add SoundRoute to your Home Screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Safari can clear a website's data after a week without a visit; Home Screen apps keep it.
            
          
            You can also link an email under Your account to use your walks on another device.
            
        
            

        
            
          
            Find something to hear
            
          
            You only hear your own walks and walks you save. Save a few before you head out.
